Project Sparky

What the hell is Sparky?

We are a non-profit organization dedicated to creating and developing free as in freedom software specifically for the gaming industry. Our mission is to empower developers and enthusiasts by providing open-source tools that enhance creativity and innovation. By prioritizing accessibility and collaboration, we aim to reshape the gaming landscape with ethical, community-driven solutions. Join us in supporting a future where gaming technology is open, transparent, and free as in freedom for everyone.
